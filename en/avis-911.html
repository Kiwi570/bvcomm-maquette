Why this notice

The CRTC requires local VoIP service providers to inform their customers, before service starts, of the availability, characteristics and limitations of their 9-1-1 or E9-1-1 service (Telecom Decisions 2005-21 and 2005-61), and to obtain their express consent where limitations exist.

What the notice must cover

The type of 9-1-1 service offered (basic or enhanced), its VoIP-specific limitations, what customers must do to account for them, and how they inform other users: to be completed by BV Communications.
