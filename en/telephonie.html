What is VoIP?

VoIP stands for Voice over Internet Protocol: phone service through the internet. The technology has moved past simply replacing traditional telephones to adding voice and video calling to messaging, driven by connected devices and a far lower cost than copper wire phone service.

Why choose VoIP?

  1. 01More than voice. You can communicate via video, text, or chat.
  2. 02No longer tied to a desk. You can reach anyone within your organization anywhere in the world at any time.
  3. 03No longer stuck with your phone company. Dynamic, flexible companies like BV Communications give your company the tools it needs to succeed.
  4. 04Savings. Do much more for much less.

3CX: the platform that grows with you.

3CX is a software-based, open-standards IP PBX with unified communications built in. It runs on your servers or in the cloud, on Windows or Linux, integrates with popular CRM systems and Microsoft Teams, and works with a wide range of IP phones, softphones and iOS and Android apps.

Licences based on simultaneous calls: quoted on request.

SIP trunking: keep your system, change your bill.

SIP trunking, or virtual phone lines, delivers superior call quality and brings Internet telephony to a wide variety of phone systems. You get the savings while protecting the investment in your current system. Our implementation team assesses adding IP trunk line cards to your system, or installs a gateway at your location giving you access to BV Communications’ private and secure SIP network.

  • Reduced costs

Frequently asked questions

Can I keep my current phone system?

Yes. With SIP trunking you keep your system and get the savings of IP telephony: our team assesses adding IP trunk line cards or installs a gateway at your location.

Can remote staff answer calls?

Yes. Voxfon includes remote extensions, and 3CX offers iOS and Android apps plus a softphone to work from anywhere.

Does it work with Microsoft Teams?

3CX integrates natively with Teams: your Teams users call 3CX users and vice versa, and can dial out through 3CX. It requires Microsoft 365 E5 with Phone System, or Business Voice.

Which phones and headsets can I use?

We offer Yealink, Grandstream and Fanvil phones, and Plantronics/Poly headsets. With 3CX, you can also choose your own IP phones.

What about 9-1-1?

Voxfon includes E911 service. The limitations of 9-1-1 on VoIP will be set out in the 9-1-1 notice, to be completed by BV Communications.
