Commission for Complaints for Telecom-television Services (CCTS)

The CCTS is an independent agency that resolves, free of charge, consumer complaints about telecom and TV services, and small business complaints about telecom services. You must first try to resolve your complaint directly with your service provider; if you cannot reach a satisfactory resolution, the CCTS may be able to help. Toll-free: 1 888 221-1687.

Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission (CRTC)

The CRTC is the public organization that regulates broadcasting and telecommunications in Canada. It took over responsibility for regulating telecommunication carriers in 1976. Toll-free: 1 877 249-2782.
